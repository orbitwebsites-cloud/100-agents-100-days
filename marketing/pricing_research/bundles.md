- **[primary]**: read on the vendor's own page.

## 5. Payment cost floor (Stripe, US)

Source: https://stripe.com/pricing [primary; page downloaded and text extracted 2026-09-27]
- Cards: **2.9% + 30¢** per successful domestic card transaction. International cards +1.5%. Currency conversion +1%. Manually entered cards +0.5%.
- Stripe Billing (pay as you go): **0.7% of Billing volume**.
- Stripe Tax: 0.5% per transaction where registered (no-code).
- Managed Payments (Stripe as merchant of record): **+3.5%** on top of payments fees.
- Comparable merchant-of-record options: Paddle **5% + 50¢** (https://www.paddle.com/pricing); Lemon Squeezy **5% + 50¢** (https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing) [primary].

Net per charge, my arithmetic from the rates above:

| Charge | Stripe card + Billing: fee (% of price) | Net | + Stripe Tax | + intl card | Stripe Managed Payments (MoR): net (fee %) | Paddle / Lemon Squeezy: net (fee %) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $4.99 | $0.48 (**9.6%**) | **$4.51** | $4.49 | $4.44 | $4.34 (13.1%) | $4.24 (15.0%) |
| $14.99 | $0.84 (5.6%) | $14.15 | $14.08 | $13.93 | $13.63 (9.1%) | $13.74 (8.3%) |
| $24.95 (5 × $4.99 on one invoice) | $1.20 (4.8%) | $23.75 | $23.63 | $23.38 | $22.88 (8.3%) | $23.20 (7.0%) |
| $29.99 | $1.38 (4.6%) | $28.61 | $28.46 | $28.16 | $27.56 (8.1%) | $27.99 (6.7%) |

The fixed 30¢ fee roughly doubles the fee rate at $4.99 compared with $29.99. If a customer holds several single-agent subscriptions, they must sit on **one** subscription with multiple items (one invoice, one card charge). Otherwise each extra $4.99 charge loses another 30¢.

## What this implies for Hundred

All items below are **inferences** from the data above, not findings.

1. **Hundred's all/single ratio is 6.0× ($29.99 / $4.99). That is about double the market band of 1.5 to 3.8×, which clusters at 2.5 to 3×.**
   - Adobe is 3.0×, JetBrains 1.5 to 3.8×, Setapp about 2.6×, Apple One 1.5 to 1.8×.
   - At 2.5 to 3.8× a $4.99 single, all-access would land at **$12.49 to $18.99**.
   - At Adobe's 3.0×, it is **$14.97**. That is exactly the founder price, which suggests the founder price is the "market-normal" all-access price rather than a deep discount.
   - Alternatively, keep all-access at $29.99. Then the single agent is priced at 17% of the bundle, a steeper step than Adobe (33%) or JetBrains (36 to 67%). The single becomes a decoy. That is a legitimate choice (Adobe's Photography plan plays a similar role), but make it on purpose.

2. **The reference price in the buyer's head is $20 for the whole AI.** ChatGPT Plus, Claude Pro, Gemini Pro, Perplexity Pro and Cursor Pro are all $20. Light AI tiers are $4.99 to $10.
   - All-Access at $29.99 costs **1.5× the assistant it plugs into**.
   - One agent at $4.99 costs the same as **all of Google AI Plus** ($4.99).
   - Market AI add-on increments run about **$7 to $10/mo** on top of a base: M365 Premium vs Family +$7; JetBrains AI Pro $10; Notion Plus to Business +$10/seat.
   - This suggests an "add-on to my AI" all-access lands most comfortably at **$9.99 to $19.99**, i.e., 50 to 100% of the $20 anchor. $29.99 fits prosumer and pro buyers, who compare with Lindy $29.99, Zapier Pro $29.99 monthly and Composio $29, better than consumers.

3. **The $14.99 price point is overloaded.** Any-5, a 7 to 10 agent category pack, and founder All-Access (100 agents) all cost $14.99. Once founder seats are visible, the pack and any-5 are dominated options, so they stop being real choices. The market ladders are spaced about 1.3 to 2× apart per step (Setapp 14.99 / 18.99 / 22.99; M365 9.99 / 12.99 / 19.99; Envato 39 / 59).
   - One ladder consistent with the 2.5 to 3× band, $1 to 1.5 to 3× steps, and 9-endings: single **$4.99**, category pack **$9.99** (2×), All-Access **$14.99 to $19.99** (3 to 4×), founder lock **$9.99 to $14.99**.
   - Any-5 could be dropped or merged into "pick a pack".

4. **Low prices carry structural churn and fee drag. Price for annual plans and bundles, not singles.**
   - Sub-$50 AI products show 23% GRR (ChartMogul 2025).
   - Low-priced apps convert trials at half the rate of high-priced ones and earn about 1/6 the year-1 value per payer (RevenueCat 2026).
   - A $4.99 charge loses 9.6% to Stripe + Billing, or 15% via a merchant of record, vs 4.6 to 6.7% at $29.99.
   - So the $4.99 single mainly serves as an entry and anchor product. Revenue should come from bundle and annual plans. An annual option (the market norm is 17 to 40% off, e.g., Claude $17 vs $20, JetBrains, Setapp "up to 40%") also removes 11 of 12 fixed 30¢ fees.

5. **Keep 9-endings, and use them where the left digit drops.** Thomas & Morwitz show the effect only when the leftmost digit changes: $4.99 vs $5, $14.99 vs $15, $19.99 vs $20, $29.99 vs $30 all qualify. Anderson & Simester found 9-endings work best for **new** items, which describes Hundred.

6. **Distribution cannot rely on the AI platforms' own stores for billing yet.** GPT Store revenue share is a closed pilot. ChatGPT Apps allow only external checkout, and in-chat payments are limited to physical goods. No paid listing mechanism was verified for the Claude directory. Per-agent willingness to pay in adjacent markets:
   - one-time prompt about $6 median (PromptBase);
   - suggested MCP server subscription $9 to $30/mo (MCPize);
   - agent platforms $19 to $30/mo for unlimited agents.

   A single deterministic agent at $4.99/mo sits at the low end of the MCP range and above the one-time prompt median. That is defensible for a single agent. For "all agents" the market comparator is the $19 to $30 agent-platform tier, not 100 × anything.
