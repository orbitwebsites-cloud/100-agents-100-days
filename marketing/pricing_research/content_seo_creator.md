# Hundred pricing research: Content & Writing, SEO & Growth, Creators & Personal

All prices were seen on **2026-09-27** unless the row says otherwise. Every row gives its source. Tags used below:
- **[official]**: the vendor's own pricing page, help page, or blog.
- **[3P]**: a third-party review or aggregator. I used one when the vendor page was blocked, rendered prices with JavaScript, or said "price on request". These are less reliable.
- **[INFERENCE]**: my own arithmetic or judgement, not a published number.

"Monthly" means the price when billed month to month. "Annual-eq" means the annual price divided by 12. Currency is USD unless marked.

## Method (applies to every category)

**Overlap weights [INFERENCE]**: each weight is the share of a tool's value that a Hundred agent can replace. The agent runs inside the customer's own AI and adds a playbook plus deterministic calculators, scorers and validators. It does not provide a UI, scheduling, publishing, stored history, or proprietary data such as keyword indexes or backlink graphs.
- **High = 0.5**: the agent produces the same core output. The customer still loses the tool's UI, workflow and storage.
- **Partial = 0.25**: the agent covers the expertise or drafting part. The tool's data, automation or publishing is still needed.
- **Low = 0.05**: the tool's value is mostly execution, hosting or data that we don't provide.

**Replaced value [INFERENCE]**: I picked one representative tool per agent job, so no job is counted twice. For each, I multiplied its annual-eq price by the overlap weight and summed the results. Then I adjusted for the fact that a typical buyer only does some of the jobs in a pack.

**Prerequisite cost**: every Hundred customer must already pay for their own AI (Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor). That subscription isn't priced here because I didn't verify it in this pass. It still lowers willingness to pay (WTP) for anything on top of it.

### Partial-substitute discount [INFERENCE]
Apply **about 40–50%** to the realistic replaced value. Reasons:
1. No scheduling or publishing. Typefully, Hypefury, Taplio and AuthoredUp all post and track analytics, and Hundred does neither.
2. The customer must already pay for their own AI. Hundred can't be priced as if it were the generator.
3. The jobs Jasper does are largely covered by the customer's own AI already. The weight above already credits only the playbook part.
4. Cheap anchors sit at $5–12/mo annual-eq: Hypefury ($6 per channel), Wordtune, Hemingway, and Grammarly on annual billing.

### Recommendation
- **Pack price: $9–15/mo. Anchor at $12/mo, or about $99/yr.** That's roughly half the $25 monthly-billing median and just under the $16 annual-eq median. It's also a 70% discount against buying the 8 paid agents à la carte ($39.92).

### Single agents at $4.99 [INFERENCE]
- **Under-priced**:
  - **blog-writer**: comparables are Jasper at $59–69 and Surfer-style content tools.
  - **linkedin-ghostwriter**: Taplio's AI tier is $49–69 and AuthoredUp is about $17–20. $6.99–7.99 is defensible.
  - **content-repurposer**: Castmagic is $19.92 and Repurpose.io starts at $29.
  - **case-study-writer**: B2B buyer, and one case study is worth a lot even if rarely produced.
- **Fair**: youtube-scripter. vidIQ's AI tier is higher, but TubeBuddy Pro is $4.99 annual-eq (low confidence).
- **Over-priced**:
  - **x-thread-builder**: Hypefury at $6/channel also schedules and posts. Typefully has a free tier.
  - **newsletter-editor**: the ESPs bundle AI into plans people already pay for.
  - **speechwriter**: episodic, one-off use doesn't fit a monthly subscription. Better included in the pack or sold as a one-time pass.

  - **programmatic-seo**: a professional strategy job; Surfer and Clearscope cost $49–129.
  - **seo-auditor**: under-priced to fair against Screaming Frog's $23/mo, but it has no crawler.
- **Fair**: growth-experiments and referral-program designer. Their comparables are execution platforms with free tiers (GrowthBook, Statsig), so there's no strong anchor. The buyer is professional and the calculators (sample size, referral unit economics) are exactly where deterministic tools add value.

### Is Creators & Personal coherent as one bundle? No [INFERENCE]
- **Different buyers and WTP.** Creators are semi-professional, often expense the cost, and their tool medians are $16–24. Personal buyers are consumers, with a median of about $6 annual-eq for these apps and RevenueCat's $10 monthly / $35–45 yearly norms.
- **Different comparison sets.** A fitness user compares us to Fitbod and MacroFactor. A podcaster compares us to Castmagic and Descript. One price will overcharge one group or undercharge the other.
- **Weakest product fit.** Personal jobs depend on stored history: workout logs, food logs, and flashcard review schedules. Hundred doesn't store data, so the partial-substitute discount is steepest here.
- **Different usage shapes.** Travel and study are episodic (a trip, a semester). Fitness and meals are recurring.

**Better groupings:**
1. **Creator Studio**: content-calendar, short-video-scripter, podcast-producer, sponsorship-pricer. Either fold these into Content & Writing, or build a creator pack with youtube-scripter, x-thread-builder and content-repurposer. The buyer overlaps almost completely with the Content buyer.
2. **Health**: fitness-coach plus meal-planner. MacroFactor already sells this pairing: Nutrition + Workouts for $89.99/yr ($7.50/mo) against $71.99/yr for one app.
3. **Life / episodic**: travel-planner and study-coach. Sell as one-time passes or use them as free acquisition agents, not a monthly subscription. Travel's median monthly price is $4.99 and Wanderlog Pro is $3.33/mo annual-eq.

### Recommendation
- **Creator Studio (4 agents): $7–10/mo.** That's about 20% below the realistic replaced value, plus the partial-substitute discount; these tools also schedule, record and edit, and we don't. If merged into Content & Writing, raise that pack by about $3, to around $15/mo.
- **Personal / Life (4 agents): $4.99–6.99/mo, or $29.99–39.99/yr.** That's at or below the RevenueCat consumer medians ($9.99 monthly; $39.94 Health & Fitness annual; $44.99 Education annual) and near MacroFactor's two-app bundle at $7.50/mo. The discount is about 40–50% against full apps, because we have no logging or state.
- **If forced to sell as one bundle: $9.99/mo.** This is not recommended. At that price, personal users will see the bundle as too expensive, and creators will get it too cheaply relative to the Content pack.
