# Part 2: Getting My First Customer — Script

**Series:** 100 Agents · 100 Days
**Video:** Part 2 — "I Built An AI Agent For My Business... Now I Need My First Customer"
**Format:** Casual talking-head vlog, screen recordings cut in, 6–9 min
**Channel:** @AgentKid23

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00–0:20)
*Handheld/selfie angle, energetic, no intro card yet.*

> "Last video I built Meeting Ops — an AI agent that takes your meeting transcript and automatically files the notes, creates the tasks, and drafts the follow-up email. It actually works. Problem is... nobody's used it but me. So today I'm getting my first real customer. On camera. Let's go."

Cut to title card: **PART 2 — FIRST CUSTOMER**

---

## RECAP (0:20–1:00)
*Sit-down, calmer.*

- 15-second recap of what Meeting Ops does (files to Notion, creates Linear tasks, drafts Gmail follow-up)
- One line on the build: "Runs on Cerebras or Claude, dry-run mode so you can see it work before connecting anything"
- The pivot: "Building it was the easy part. This part — actually getting someone to use it — is the part everyone skips in these videos. I'm not skipping it."

---

## THE PROBLEM (1:00–2:00)
*Walk-and-talk or desk shot.*

- Be honest: zero customers, zero audience beyond this channel
- State the target: 1 real person, not a friend doing you a favor — someone who runs actual meetings (freelancer, small agency, PM) and has a real transcript
- Show the plan on screen (whiteboard/notes app):
  1. List 10 people who run meetings for a living
  2. DM/message 10 of them with a specific, no-fluff offer
  3. Get 1 to actually run their transcript through it
  4. Watch them use it, get their honest reaction on camera (with permission)

---

## SCREEN SECTION: THE OUTREACH (2:00–3:30)
*Screen recording, voiceover.*

- Show the actual DM template being written live, e.g.:

> "Hey [name] — I built a free tool that turns your meeting transcript into filed notes + tasks + a drafted follow-up email automatically. Takes 30 seconds, no signup needed to try it. Want me to run your last meeting through it and send you the output?"

- Send it to 10 real people (blur last names/handles if needed for privacy)
- Show replies coming in — include at least one rejection/silence, don't fake 100% success. Authenticity sells the series.

---

## SCREEN SECTION: THE DEMO (3:30–5:30)
*Screen recording of `python run.py --transcript theirfile.txt`, or a live call.*

- Take the one person who said yes
- Run their real transcript through Meeting Ops live
- Show the output: Notion page, Linear tasks, Gmail draft
- Cut to their reaction — genuine, unscripted

> "Wait, it actually pulled out my action items correctly?"

---

## THE CLOSE (5:30–7:00)
*Back to talking head.*

- Ask directly, on camera: "Would you actually use this every week?"
- If yes: "That's customer #1." Get a name/testimonial line you can use (with permission) — this becomes social proof for Part 3
- If it's a partial yes / needs work: be honest about what needs fixing — this sets up Part 3 as "fixing what my first customer told me was broken"

---

## OUTRO / CTA (7:00–7:45)

> "That's one real person using something I built from scratch, on camera, no fake numbers. Next video: either I get customer #2, or I fix what's broken based on what [name] told me. Sub if you want to watch me actually build this into a real business instead of just another AI demo video. Meeting Ops is free and open — link's in the description if you want to try it on your own meetings."

End card: subscribe + link to repo + "Part 3 next."
